## Research interests

- Emotional processing and the experience dimension of inhibitory control
- Childhood emotional abuse and emotional neglect
- Measurement adaptation and cultural validity in Indian samples

## Current work

Undergraduate thesis, supervised by **Dr Simantini Ghosh**, Department of
Psychology, Ashoka University. Testing whether childhood emotional abuse and
emotional neglect relate differently to inhibitory control in Indian emerging
adults, using an emotional Go/No-Go paradigm and the Traumatic Antecedents
Questionnaire.
